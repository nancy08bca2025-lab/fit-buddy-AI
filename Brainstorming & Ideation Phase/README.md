# Brainstorming & Ideation Phase

## Project Title
Fit Buddy AI

## Problem Statement
Many people find it difficult to maintain a proper fitness routine and track their daily fitness activities.

## Proposed Solution
Fit Buddy AI is a fitness support application that helps users follow a personalized fitness plan and maintain their fitness activities.

## Objectives
- Help users maintain a regular fitness routine.
- Provide suitable fitness guidance.
- Make fitness tracking simple and easy.
- Encourage users to stay consistent.

## Target Users
- Students
- Beginners
- People interested in fitness

## Expected Outcome
A simple and user-friendly fitness application that helps users manage their fitness activities.
